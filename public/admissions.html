Our Network

Our Accredited University Network

Every institution and course we recommend is filtered against a simple standard: is it recognized, is the degree valid for jobs and higher studies, and does it fit the way you want to study. Here's what sits behind that network, and what "verified" actually means at AVM Academy.

How the network works

AVM Academy is partnered with College Vidya, which gives us direct access to a wide network of UGC-approved universities offering online, distance, and hybrid degree programs. We are an independent advisory layer on top of that network — we don't run the universities ourselves, and we're transparent about that relationship so you know exactly who you're dealing with at each step.

What "verified" means

Before any course is added to our shortlist pool, we check UGC approval status, confirm the degree is valid for government job eligibility and higher education (like postgraduate admissions or competitive exams), and review the university's track record on things like exam conduct, result timelines, and placement support. We update this regularly rather than treating it as a one-time check.

Modes of study

Depending on the university, you can choose fully online (self-paced or live-class formats), distance learning (correspondence-based with periodic contact classes), or hybrid programs that mix online modules with occasional on-campus sessions. We'll walk you through which mode suits your schedule, especially if you're working full-time or managing other commitments.

Degree tracks we cover

BBA BCA B.Com B.Tech Executive / MBA-track programs

Whether you're a 12th-grade graduate exploring your first degree or a working professional looking at an executive program to move up in your career, we match you against tracks built for your stage, not a one-size-fits-all list.

What the enrollment process looks like

  1. We shortlist 3–5 verified programs based on your profile (see our Guidance page for that process).
  2. You review fee structure, syllabus, and accreditation details for each option — no pressure to decide on the spot.
  3. Once you choose, we help you complete the university's official application and document submission.
  4. We track your admission confirmation and share it with you directly, along with your student login details.
  5. A mentor stays reachable after enrollment in case you need help during the semester.
