How We Guide You

Our Mentorship Framework, Explained

Choosing a degree after 12th shouldn't feel like guesswork. Here's exactly what happens at each stage of working with AVM Academy — what we ask of you, what we do behind the scenes, and what you walk away with.

01

Profile Mapping

We start with a short conversation, not a sales pitch. A mentor asks about your 12th-standard stream (Commerce, Science, or Arts), the subjects you actually enjoyed, your realistic budget range, and whether you're balancing this with a job, family responsibilities, or another course. We also ask about longer-term goals — do you want to work while studying, are you targeting a specific industry, is a particular city or mode of study (online, hybrid, or distance) a constraint for you.

This isn't a form you fill and forget — it's a two-way conversation, and it's the step that prevents the most common mistake students make: picking a "popular" degree that doesn't actually fit their situation.

02

Strategic Shortlisting

Using your profile, we shortlist 3–5 university and course combinations from our partner network — never a single "take it or leave it" option. Each recommendation comes with a plain-language breakdown of total fees, accreditation status (UGC-approved, and any additional NAAC/NBA recognition where relevant), typical time to complete the degree, and what the course actually covers semester by semester.

We deliberately avoid recommending programs based on which one pays us the most — our shortlist is built around fit for you first. You're welcome to ask us to explain why any option was or wasn't included.

03

Fast-Track Enrollment

Once you've picked a program, we handle the admission workflow directly — document checklist, application submission, fee-payment guidance, and confirmation tracking — so you're not chasing multiple university portals or waiting on hold with call centers. A dedicated mentor stays with you from application to your first day of class, and remains a point of contact if anything comes up during the semester.

A note on how we work

AVM Academy is an independent advisory platform, not a university. We partner with College Vidya to access a wider network of UGC-approved institutions, and our mentors are trained to give you honest comparisons rather than push whichever program is easiest to close. If a traditional on-campus option or a different advisor would genuinely serve you better, we'll tell you that too.
